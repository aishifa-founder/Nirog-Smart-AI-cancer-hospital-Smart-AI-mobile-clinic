**Project Nirog** is a sovereign public health utility grid engineered to eliminate traditional outpatient waiting queues, eradicate predatory diagnostic billing, and deliver real-time, AI-driven oncology care across 8 specialized zonal hospital hubs and 1,000 mobile diagnostic smart vans for large universal screening spaces throughout India.

---

## 🏗️ System Architecture & Complete AI Stack


* **Core Cloud Infrastructure:** Deployed across sovereign dual-regions (`asia-south1` Mumbai and `asia-south2` Delhi) on **Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE)** and **Compute Engine C3 Confidential Computing**.
* **Multi-Agent & Orchestration Layer:** Powered by **Certified Multi-Agent Developer Agent Development Kit (ADK)**, **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, **Antigravity** workflow orchestration, and **Boardfly** governance modules.
* **High-Speed Ingestion & Storage:** Driven by a **250Hz Multi-Agent System (MAS) Ingestion Sync** and **Dual-SSD Bigtable** delivering ultra-low fetch latency of **0.74 ms**.
* **Clinical Intelligence & Specialized Models:** Utilizes **MedGemma 7B** specialized across 40 cancer phenotypes (26 adult + 14 pediatric), alongside advanced model execution via **Argon4** and eco-compute tracking through **Carbon4AI**.
* **Field Telemetry & Mobile Grid:** Integrates **FieldAI**, **FastTrack3D** spatial modeling, **HealthConnect**, Satellite connectivity, GPS tracking, Solar, and **Hydrogen4** energy modules for unyielding rural operations.
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Built natively for India's **DPDP Act 2023**, **ABDM/ABHA** token layers, and **HL7 FHIR R4** standards.

## 💰 Cost Savings & Economic Impact Model

Project Nirog replaces legacy administrative bloat, predatory diagnostic markups, and costly outpatient delays with absolute financial and operational efficiency:

* **Predatory Billing Eradication:** Algorithmic verification and multi-agent validation intercept unauthorized diagnostic markups and duplicate scans, saving **₹59,99,70,000 ($\approx$ ₹60 Crores)** in direct patient billing inefficiencies ($\text{₹0.00}$ unauthorized charges).
* **Outpatient Queue Reclamation:** Reduces legacy 4.5-hour outpatient wait times to **0.00s** queue delay via 90-second automated triage loops, reclaiming **571,400 cumulative clinical hours** for patients and their families.
* **Capital Expenditure (CapEx) Optimization:** The hybrid zonal model (8 sterile, positive-pressure cleanroom hospital hubs paired with 1,000 decentralized mobile smart units) bypasses the multi-billion-dollar cost of building traditional general mega-hospitals across every district while eliminating nosocomial cross-infection risks for immunocompromised patients.
